The other direction: start at the ideal key

Ideal signs: . These are the first flip sets in increasing total penalty. A set can skip a cheaper bit; it isn't just a growing prefix.

Penalty = sum of |query value| for flipped bits. Binary score = sum of |query values| − 2 × penalty. A lookup still has a cost when no document has that key.

06 / QUALITY ASSUMPTIONS

A distribution is an assumption, not ground truth

For short-key and substring probing, assume a relevant document independently disagrees with each query sign with probability p. This gives a toy probability of including that document. It is not measured nearest-neighbor recall or answer accuracy. A normal distribution alone does not tell us p.

Short key: P[Binomial(key bits, p) ≤ radius]. Multiple substrings: 1 − product of each substring's miss probability, assuming independence. Outer-cluster misses are not included. There is no corresponding justified real-data recall formula for the branch or prefilter controls.

Sources and boundaries

Exa's vector database uses similarity clusters and float-query × binary-document scoring. It does not describe a first-bits router. Our scanner is a local reference, not Exa's optimized production implementation.

Multi-index hashing searches binary substrings. Its Hamming guarantees need care when query dimensions have different weights.
