Where the estimates come from

A: fitted to our scan timings. B: uses saved counts of documents scored and bitmap words visited. Changing group size scales the closest saved work example; changing bits reuses its branch counts. These are assumptions, not new benchmark results.

C: assumes equally likely, independent document bits and allows 65,536 key attempts per opened group. The lookup and queue costs are unmeasured. This tells us about empty-key work; it cannot predict actual recall.

Grouping adds an assumed 0.05 ms. All methods use cached queries, one CPU thread, and target 100 candidates. Embedding, building the index, final float reranking and network time are outside the calculation.

“Extra stored data” excludes queues and temporary memory. B can queue many full-group masks. These are not process memory measurements.
